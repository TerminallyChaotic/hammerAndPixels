Privacy Policy

Varnish, by Hammer & Pixels · Last updated: April 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Hammer & Pixels ("we", "us") handles your information when you use the Varnish mobile app on iOS, Android, and the web.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information. When you create an account or sign in, we collect your email address, a display name (optional), and a securely hashed password. Authentication is handled by our identity provider, Webway.
  • App content. Paints you mark as owned, projects, recipes, mood boards, bookmarks, notes, and similar content you create in the app are stored on our servers so they can sync across your devices.
  • Camera input. The app uses your device camera for two features only: scanning paint barcodes and sampling colors. Camera frames are processed entirely on-device and are never uploaded, recorded, or transmitted off your phone.
  • Diagnostic data. If the app crashes, anonymized crash reports may be collected to help us fix bugs. These reports do not include your account content.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To sync your paints, projects, and other app content across your devices.
  • To deliver app features you request (e.g., barcode lookup, color matching).
  • To send transactional emails such as password resets and email verification.
  • To diagnose and fix bugs.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers strictly necessary to operate the app:

  • Webway (identity provider) — handles account authentication and session tokens.
  • Hosting and database providers — store your synced app content.
  • Apple and Google — when you sign in with Apple ID or Google, those services authenticate you on our behalf and provide a token.

4. Camera and Photos

Camera access is requested only when you explicitly use the barcode scanner or the color match feature. Image data is processed locally on your device and discarded after each use. We do not retain, upload, or share captured images.

5. Children

Varnish is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account and app content for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated content at any time by emailing us at hello@hammerandpixels.com. Upon request, we will permanently delete your account, content, and any associated data within 30 days.

7. Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your data, including TLS encryption in transit and encrypted at-rest storage of authentication credentials. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, email us at hello@hammerandpixels.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change. Material changes will be communicated through the app or via email.

10. Contact

Questions or requests? Email us at hello@hammerandpixels.com.
