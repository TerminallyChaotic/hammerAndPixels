Our story

A short menu, cooked over oak

Embers & Oak is built around a single wood-fired hearth and a kitchen that believes less is more. The menu is short on purpose — a handful of seasonal dishes, each one the best version of itself we can cook that night.

Our oak comes from a family sawmill an hour up the road. Our bread is baked in house. Our wine list is small, considered, and mostly from growers you haven't heard of — which is exactly the point.

— The kitchen at Embers & Oak
